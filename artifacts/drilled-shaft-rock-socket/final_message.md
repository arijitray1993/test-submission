I've drafted the memo to DeLuca for your signature, dated May 15, 2026 (5 pages). The short answer is that the Bent 2 shafts are not adequate as designed, and the shortfall comes from our design, not from site conditions.

**Why they aren't adequate:** The design needs 8 ft of socket in sound limestone. The plan tip of 504.3 ft is 8 ft below 512.3 ft, but 512.3 ft is where the auger hit weathered rock at Boring B-7. Redoak's April 21 addendum puts the top of sound limestone at 509.4 ft. That leaves only 5.1 ft of socket in sound rock; the tip needed to be at 501.4 ft or lower.
- Redoak's 2024 report had already said the tip should be at about 502.1 ft or lower.
- Our own scour report worked out only 5.8 ft in sound rock and still called it adequate.
- The scour floor at Bent 2 was set on the weathered rock, which is the layer most likely to erode.

**It isn't a differing site condition.** Our dailies show rock at about 512.0 ft, within 0.3 ft of the boring. Vantera's 518.4 ft has no support in the record, and CO-06 already paid for slow drilling in hard rock at Bent 2. We also can't charge the shortfall to Vantera: it drilled to the plan tip, our inspector called the rock sound, and our resident engineer accepted the tip. The shaft was concreted 03/25, and the column and cap are now built on it.
