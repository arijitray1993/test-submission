**Why the two 2025 surveys can't be compared as labelled.** Merrowfield measures heights from the top of the ringwall. Ashgrove measures from the bottom-of-shell weld, which its procedure says is 12 in lower. So the two "C1-S-6" readings are 12 in apart on the shell. The inspection report's 0.031 in "step" is the thickness change over that 12 in, not corrosion. Read as loss, it would mean 83 mpy and reaching the minimum by 10/2026. The readings themselves confirm the offset:
- At the eight points both contractors actually measured, they agree within 0.002 in.
- On the sister tanks T-02-001 and T-02-002, Ashgrove read at Merrowfield's points and matched within 0.000–0.004 in.
