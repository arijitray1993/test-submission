# Agent's final message

I've drafted the memo for Dana's signature and saved it as `/filesystem/Span2_Haunch_Verification.docx` (4 pages, dated May 21, 2026). The answer is **no**: the Span 2 build-up in the approved girder package can't be used as submitted.

**Why it fails against the contract documents**
- **Wrong elevations.** The elevations the package says come from Sheet B-01 are 1.79–1.80 ft higher than B-01 at every support.
- **Wrong profile.** B-01 is a straight −0.5% grade, so the deck should drop 0.52 ft across Span 2. The package's table drops 0.12 ft and dips in the middle.
- **Camber backwards.** The table shows the girders sagging 5.0 in. at midspan, but the package's own camber diagram has them cambering 3.60 in. upward. That's why its haunch grows to 6.1 in. at midspan instead of shrinking.
- **No slab deflection and no screed elevations.** The 2.10 in. the girders drop under the wet deck isn't accounted for anywhere.
- **Bearing haunch wrong.** The table shows 2.0 and 1.9 in.; the contract documents give 3.5 in. The 1.9 in. is also below the package's own 2.0-in. minimum.
- **The approval doesn't cover it.** Larkspur's approval was limited to strand patterns, losses, camber and end-zone reinforcement, not the haunch table. Shop drawings aren't contract documents and can't override B-01.
